About N.F International

Leading Bangladesh's Manpower Export Excellence

Founded with a vision to empower Bangladeshi workforce on the global stage, N.F International is a fully government-licensed overseas recruitment agency headquartered in Dhaka. We specialize in providing complete, ethical, and efficient human resource solutions for international enterprises and government contractors.

With years of industry expertise, our team bridges the gap between ambitious job seekers looking for reliable overseas careers and foreign employers seeking trustworthy, trade-tested professionals.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class manpower recruitment services with unwavering integrity, compliance, and candidate welfare.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as Bangladesh's most dependable and transparent human capital export partner.

End-to-End Solutions

Comprehensive Recruitment Services

From initial talent sourcing and trade testing to visa stamping and flight logistics, we handle every stage of manpower deployment.

Manpower Supply

Provision of highly skilled professionals, semi-skilled technicians, and general labor for construction, healthcare, hospitality, and engineering sectors.

  • Customized candidate sourcing
  • Bulk recruitment capabilities
  • Pre-employment background checks

Visa & Documentation

Complete management of embassy attestation, visa processing, BMET smart card clearance, and official government approvals.

  • Embassy document attestation
  • BMET clearance & insurance
  • Rapid visa stamping

Screening & Training

Rigorous practical trade testing at certified technical institutes alongside orientation on destination country laws, safety, and culture.

  • Hands-on skill evaluation
  • Pre-departure briefing
  • Basic language & safety training

Travel & Logistics Support

End-to-end flight booking, transit assistance, and airport escorting to ensure seamless deployment of candidate groups.

  • Group air ticketing
  • Dhaka airport departure assistance
  • Destination arrival coordination

Post-Deployment Support

Continuous follow-up with employers and workers to safeguard candidate welfare, contract compliance, and long-term satisfaction.

  • Employer-worker liaison
  • 24/7 candidate assistance
  • Contract renewal support
